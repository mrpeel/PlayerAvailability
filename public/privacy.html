Privacy Policy

Laburnum Cricket Club Availability & Team Selection System

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview

The Laburnum Cricket Club Availability & Team Selection System ("the Application", "we", "our") is an internal club administration tool created to manage match availability, team selection, and roster communications for registered players and team selectors of Laburnum Cricket Club (Melbourne, Victoria, Australia).

We respect the privacy of all club members, players, and volunteers. This Privacy Policy details how the Application accesses, uses, stores, and protects member information and Google user data.

2. Information We Collect and Process

The Application processes the following limited information exclusively for club match operations:

  • Player Profile & Contact Information: Player names, PlayHQ profile identifiers, registered phone numbers, and emergency/parent contact numbers. These are sourced directly from official PlayHQ club registration exports and team communications.
  • Match Availability Data: Player responses indicating availability (Available / Unavailable), specific match dates, and optional notes (e.g., late arrival, injury context) submitted via the mobile check-in interface.
  • Player Profile Headshots: Club-captured portrait photos used for standardized visual avatars on team selection sheets and presentation slides.

3. Google User Data & API Scopes

The Application integrates with Google Workspace APIs (Google Sheets, Google Slides, and Google Drive) via Google Apps Script to maintain club records and generate match-day team presentations.

The specific Google API scopes requested and their purposes are:

  • Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets): Used to read match fixtures, player rosters, and record player availability responses. It also allows authorized selectors to move players between team selections.
  • Google Slides (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/presentations): Used to synchronize selected team rosters, opponents, venues, and player avatar images into the club's Google Slides match-day presentation deck.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive / drive.file): Used strictly to save and retrieve cropped player headshot images within the club's designated Google Drive storage folder.

The Application only accesses documents and presentations explicitly designated for Laburnum Cricket Club operations. It does not access, read, or modify any user's personal emails, unrelated files, or external drive contents.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Google Limited Use Compliance:
The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide and improve the club availability and selection features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties unless necessary to provide the service or comply with applicable law.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • We do not permit humans to read Google user data, except where authorized club selectors review player rosters or when required for technical troubleshooting with user consent.

5. How Data is Shared

We never sell, rent, monetize, or share member information with third parties.

Player availability and selected team lists are shared strictly within the club community (e.g., team announcements sent to players via team WhatsApp groups and published on club social presentation slides).

6. Data Storage and Security

All player data and team selections reside inside the club's private Google Workspace environment (Google Sheets and Google Drive) protected by Google's industry-standard enterprise security.

Requests between the web interface and Google Sheets are proxied securely using encrypted HTTPS connections (TLS) via Firebase Cloud Functions.

7. Data Retention and Member Rights

Availability logs and roster data are maintained for the duration of the relevant cricket season to facilitate team selection, historical match records, and trophy/qualification eligibility.

Club members have the right to inspect, update, or request the deletion of their contact numbers and profile images at any time by contacting club administration.
