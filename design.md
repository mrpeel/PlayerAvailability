# Laburnum Cricket Club Design System

## Core Identity
The Heritage Athletic System is designed to balance the historical weight of a club established in 1894 with the functional requirements of a modern, mobile-first utility app used outdoors.

## Visual Language
- **Palette**: Deep Maroon and Bright Gold provide high contrast against the light cream surface, ensuring the UI remains readable in direct sunlight.
- **Typography**: Uses 'Hanken Grotesk' for a professional yet approachable athletic feel. Minimum font sizes are enforced at 16px.
- **Interactions**: Large, high-contrast buttons and radio selectors prioritize tap accuracy for players checking in while on the field.

## Platform Constraints
- **WhatsApp Webview**: Optimized for low-latency loading within the inbuilt WhatsApp browser.
- **Flat Design**: Avoids heavy gradients or transparency layers to maintain contrast and performance.
